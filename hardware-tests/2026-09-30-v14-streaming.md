## Controlled detection test

| Word | Times spoken | Detections | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| STOP | 10 | 3 | 7 misses |
| START | 5 | 4 | 4 false activations |
| SHORT | 5 | 0 | No natural-pronunciation false activations |
| STARK | 5 | 2 | 2 false activations |
| STOT | 5 | 5 | 5 false activations |
| SHOP | 5 | 0 | No false activations |

An additional exaggerated SHORT with a strongly emphasized final T triggered
one stream; it is not included in the natural SHORT count. Extra unstructured
word testing after the SHOP block produced activations #24 and #25 and is not
included in the controlled table.

This short same-speaker test confirms that V14 is not a finished accurate
detector. In this run it missed most intended STOP utterances and remained
strongly confused by START and STOT.
